THE REIGN OF TERROR

Paris, 1793–1794
5:19 runtimeDeep British narrator, acted character voicesBlack and white, only the red keptCaptions and word cards burned in

Five parts: a republic in danger, anyone can be on the list, the Incorruptible, the Revolution turns on its own, and the last name. Every hard word is explained as it comes up and shown on a “word to know” card.

The question it ends on

Do the ends justify the means?

Robespierre's goal was to save a free France, full of good and honest citizens, from its enemies. He used Rousseau's idea that people can be “forced to be free” to justify the killing. His method was terror.

What happens

  • Dec 1792 – Jan 1793: Louis XVI is tried and executed. Robespierre: "Louis must die, because the nation must live."
  • Spring 1793: War with much of Europe and a rebellion in the Vendée. The Committee of Public Safety is created; Robespierre joins in July.
  • 5 Sept 1793: "Make terror the order of the day."
  • 17 Sept 1793: The Law of Suspects. About 300,000 people are arrested during the Terror.
  • 16 Oct 1793: Marie Antoinette is guillotined.
  • 5 Feb 1794: Robespierre: "Terror is nothing but justice: swift, severe, and unbending."
  • 5 April 1794: Danton and Desmoulins are guillotined.
  • 10 June 1794: The law of 22 Prairial begins the Great Terror.
  • 27–28 July 1794: 9 Thermidor: the Convention turns on Robespierre. He is shot in the jaw and guillotined the next evening.
