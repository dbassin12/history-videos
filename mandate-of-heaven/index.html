Teacher notes

  • The Qin and the Mandate. The First Emperor's own stone inscriptions never mention Heaven's support, so the video says the Qin “united China” and has the Han claim the Mandate. Good question for class: why would a ruler skip the Mandate? (Yuri Pines, The Messianic Emperor.)
  • Late meant death?. Sima Qian says it did. Qin law texts found at Shuihudi in 1975 show fines for lateness and duty canceled in heavy rain. Sima Qian wrote under the Han, the dynasty that replaced the Qin.
  • Dates. The video uses 1046 BCE for Muye, the most cited date. The worksheet says 1045, which some scholars prefer. The Warring States ran about 475 to 221 BCE; the worksheet's 770 to 453 BCE describes the Spring and Autumn period.
  • Confucius and Mencius. Confucius died in 479 BCE, before the Warring States began. Mencius (about 372 to 289 BCE) was a later follower.
  • Mencius's words. Legge translates “Heaven sees as my people see.” The video says “the people” because Mencius is quoting an older text, not speaking as a ruler.
  • The drums. The battle drums are Japanese taiko recordings, used as stand-in war drums. The guqin, guzheng, erhu, gong and Suzhou drum are Chinese.

Words to know

Mandate of Heaven天命
The belief that Heaven favors one dynasty to rule China, as long as it rules well.
Heaven天
The power that controls human destiny. Not a place.
Son of Heaven天子
The title of the Zhou kings and later emperors.
Dynasty
A family that rules for generations.
Dynastic cycle
The pattern of rise, prosperity, decline, chaos and a new dynasty.
Warring States戰國
About 250 years of war among China's states, ending in 221 BCE.
Hundred Schools of Thought百家
The many ideas about how to bring back order.
Confucianism
Rulers should lead by good example.
Daoism
Live simply, in harmony with nature, and rule as little as possible.
Legalism
Control people with strict laws and harsh punishments.
Yellow Turbans黃巾
A huge peasant rebellion against the Han in 184 CE.
Blue Heaven, Yellow Heaven蒼天 黃天
In the Yellow Turbans' slogan, the Blue Heaven stood for the Han and the Yellow Heaven for the rebels.
Manchus
A people from beyond the Great Wall who conquered China in 1644 and ruled as the Qing.
